# Atlas GPT-Image Super Prompts

These prompts are tuned for iterative mascot exploration with `gpt-image-1.5`.

Use them in sequence:

1. silhouette round
2. portrait round
3. sticker round
4. pixel round

For best results, do not ask for everything at once. Generate one line at a time, then refine.

## Reusable instruction block

Paste this at the front when needed:

```text
Design original mascot creature concept art for Atlas, a premium iOS app with a calm, polished, blue-toned visual identity. The art should feel premium, elegant, and collectible, with strong silhouette clarity and readable forms that can later be simplified into icons and tiny pixel sprites. Do not make it feel like a direct Pokemon copy, a childish toy, a horror monster, or a chaotic fantasy creature. Keep the background minimal and presentation-focused, like a professional creature concept sheet.
```

```text

1. Cindlet
2. Voltflare
3. Aetherion

A storm-forged drake guardian that begins as compressed potential and evolves into a sleek celestial thunder-dragon.

Required traits across all forms:
- glowing chest core area clearly placed in the center of the torso
- horned drake head silhouette
- forward-leaning drake posture

- Cindlet: compact body, oversized forearms, tiny wing buds, large head, scrappy and underdeveloped but clearly powerful later
- Voltflare: leaner, faster, athletic limbs, sharper horns, more active wings, diagonal motion silhouette
- Aetherion: sleek legendary body, long ceremonial wings, atlas-ring halo motif, noble and calm mythic presence

Composition:
- show multiple silhouette options for each form
- arrange like a professional creature ideation sheet
- use only black, white, and grayscale
- no rendering, no color, no environment

Avoid:
- bulky western dragon
- red fire dragon clichés
- demon spikes
- goofy baby dragon proportions
```

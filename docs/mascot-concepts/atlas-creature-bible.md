# Atlas Creature Bible

Grounded in `/Users/donghokang/Developer/Atlas` on April 11, 2026.

This is the first real design artifact for mascot production. Its job is to lock the creatures as systems before prompt writing or feature implementation.

## Core Art Strategy

Atlas should support three asset modes for the same creatures:

1. polished illustrated character portraits
2. simplified icon / sticker assets
3. tiny pixel mini-sprites

That means each mascot must be designed for silhouette clarity first, surface detail second.

## Global Style Rules

### Primary rendering style

- polished 2D digital illustration
- premium mobile-game creature art
- clean silhouette edges
- soft cel shading with restrained painterly finish
- expressive but not exaggerated faces
- noble, aspirational tone over toy-like cuteness

### Brand fit

- keep the Atlas visual language: navy, cobalt, pearl, ice blue, glassy light surfaces
- use accent colors sparingly so the creatures still feel premium in a medical-adjacent product
- avoid loud saturated rainbow effects
- avoid chaotic fantasy-monster detailing

### Pixel-readability rules

Every form must survive reduction to a small sprite.

Non-negotiables:

- one unmistakable silhouette trait per line
- one central chest marker per line
- no tiny micro-details that carry core identity
- ears, wings, tail, and chest core must read in solid shape before line detail

## Asset Targets

### Portrait assets

Used in onboarding, Today, Insights, and celebrations.

Requirements:

- 3/4 view preferred
- strong chest visibility
- clear face read at phone size
- subtle atmosphere and aura allowed

### Sticker / icon assets

Used in compact UI states.

Requirements:

- front or near-front view
- simplified outlines
- no background dependency
- chest trait and silhouette must still identify the form instantly

### Pixel mini-sprites

Used in widgets and celebration moments.

Requirements:

- readable at very small sizes
- no more than 3-5 major color clusters
- one clear idle pose and one clear happy pose
- each evolution must still be distinguishable in silhouette alone

## Line A: Aetherion

Working evolution names:

1. `Cindlet`
2. `Voltflare`
3. `Aetherion`

Locked visual direction:

- use the approved image-3 portrait direction as the master reference for this line
- preserve the compact starter, athletic middle form, and noble final guardian proportions from that pass
- preserve the chest-core prominence, cobalt energy seams, and atlas-ring halo treatment from that pass
- treat future sticker and pixel work as simplifications of that approved master, not as redesigns

This line should feel like compressed power becoming celestial dominance.

### Creature identity

- species: storm-forged drake guardian
- emotional lane: disciplined, aspirational, mythic
- progression fantasy: weak-looking starter with obvious final-form destiny

### Signature traits

These must exist in all three forms:

- glowing chest core
- forward-leaning drake stance
- horned head silhouette
- dark body with luminous blue energy accents

### Silhouette logic

- Form 1: compact circle-plus-triangle silhouette
- Form 2: lean diagonal silhouette with wing extension
- Form 3: broad legendary silhouette with halo arc and major wing spread

### Anatomy

#### Form 1: Cindlet

- compact torso
- oversized forearms / paws
- small hind legs
- tiny wing buds
- large head relative to body
- chest core large compared with total body mass

The feeling should be:

- underdeveloped but dangerous later
- more scrappy than cute
- visibly holding more power than it can express

#### Form 2: Voltflare

- body length increases
- limbs become athletic and raptor-like
- wing shapes become functional but not yet majestic
- horns sharpen
- tail becomes more active and energy-driven

The feeling should be:

- speed
- discipline
- momentum

#### Form 3: Aetherion

- torso becomes sleek, not bulky
- wings become long and ceremonial
- halo or atlas-ring motif frames the upper body
- horns become elegant rather than beastly
- chest core looks controlled and internal, not explosive

The feeling should be:

- mythic guardian
- celestial thunder-dragon
- cool-headed power

### Color direction

- base body: obsidian navy, midnight blue, charcoal-blue
- energy accents: electric blue, cobalt, faint ice glow
- core accent: ember-gold to white-hot amber
- avoid bright red as a primary body color

### Face direction

- sharp eyes, not angry eyes
- expression should read confident and alert
- Form 1 can be curious
- Form 3 should read noble and calm

### What changes by evolution

- wings: buds -> active fins / wings -> ceremonial legendary wings
- horns: blunt -> sharpened -> sculptural
- stance: compressed -> forward-drive -> anchored authority
- chest core: raw ember -> brighter contained current -> luminous heart reactor

### What must never change

- chest core placement
- horned drake identity
- premium cool-toned palette
- disciplined rather than feral personality

### What to avoid

- bulky western dragon proportions
- goofy baby-dragon proportions
- red/orange fire-dragon cliches
- demonic spikes or villain energy
- overdesigned armor plating

## Line B: Aurielle

Working evolution names:

1. `Moppet`
2. `Glisshare`
3. `Aurielle`

This line should feel like softness becoming elegance without losing warmth.

### Creature identity

- species: aurora hare guardian
- emotional lane: charming, resilient, regal
- progression fantasy: adorable beginning with a graceful celestial payoff

### Signature traits

These must exist in all three forms:

- long-ear silhouette
- pearl or crescent chest mark
- soft pale body with cool luminous accents
- poised, upward posture

### Silhouette logic

- Form 1: rounded upright silhouette with oversized ears
- Form 2: taller oval silhouette with flowing ears and a visible tail sweep
- Form 3: regal vertical silhouette with long aurora ears and crescent-tail curve

### Anatomy

#### Form 1: Moppet

- rounded head
- short body
- oversized ears
- small forepaws gathered toward the chest
- chest gem or crescent-pearl mark

The feeling should be:

- immediately cute
- clean and iconic
- soft without becoming plush-toy mush

#### Form 2: Glisshare

- neck and torso elongate
- limbs get cleaner and more graceful
- ears feel ribbon-like in motion
- tail becomes visible as a design element

The feeling should be:

- poise
- brightness
- confidence

#### Form 3: Aurielle

- tall, elegant body
- long ears with aurora-like flow
- crescent or halo motif around head or chest
- tail becomes a graceful moon-arc
- posture feels serene and high-status

The feeling should be:

- celestial snow-hare guardian
- beautiful but not fragile
- elegant final form that still feels approachable

### Color direction

- base fur: pearl white, moon white, very light frost gray
- accent colors: powder blue, icy cyan, blush pink used carefully
- luminous effects: aurora blue, pale teal, soft pearl glow
- avoid candy pink dominance

### Face direction

- large clear eyes
- expression reads bright and calm
- Form 1 can be playful
- Form 3 should read serene, wise, and graceful

### What changes by evolution

- body height: compact -> poised -> regal
- ears: oversized soft ears -> ribbon motion -> aurora fins / ceremonial ears
- tail: tiny softness -> visible sweep -> crescent signature
- chest mark: gem -> brighter pearl -> celestial emblem

### What must never change

- long-ear silhouette
- chest pearl / crescent identity
- cool premium palette
- gentle but resilient emotional read

### What to avoid

- generic anime bunny-girl energy
- over-frilly magical-girl design
- plush-only proportions in later forms
- too much pink
- hyper-real fur rendering

## Cross-Line Differentiation

These two lines must feel intentionally paired.

Shared system:

- both are guardian creatures
- both use a chest-centered identity marker
- both evolve from compact to aspirational
- both fit the Atlas palette and premium tone

Key difference:

- `Aetherion` line expands outward into power
- `Aurielle` line rises upward into elegance

Short version:

- male line: compressed -> aggressive motion -> mythic dominance
- female line: soft -> poised -> celestial grace

## Sprite Readability Checklist

Before any art direction is approved, it should pass this test:

- can I identify the line in silhouette only?
- can I identify the form from the body proportions alone?
- can I still find the chest trait at tiny size?
- can the final forms be told apart without color?

If the answer is no, the design is too complicated.

## Production Direction

Use this order:

1. silhouette sheets
2. black-and-white value passes
3. color passes
4. portrait concepts
5. sticker simplification
6. pixel mini adaptation

Do not start with rendered beauty shots. Start with shape language.
